How it works

From a single portrait to a living character.

Cast

A single portrait becomes a face that blinks, breathes, reacts and speaks, animated right in your browser.

Voice

Natural voices, chosen and auditioned for each character, with accents that fit the character's own story.

Scene

Every character has a personality, a goal and a mood that changes with how the conversation goes.

Reflect

Practice scenes end with friendly, specific coaching: what worked, and what to try next time.

Private by design

The animation runs on your device, so faces never leave it. Conversations are not recorded, and coaching notes are deleted as soon as they are shown.

Respectful casting

A character's accent follows where they grew up and the languages they speak, never their appearance. Voices aim for authenticity, never caricature.
